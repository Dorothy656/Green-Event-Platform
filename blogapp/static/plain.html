About Green Event Platform

The Green Event Platform is a community-driven web application that helps people turn environmental awareness into real actions. By making it easy to create and join local green events, we hope to lower the barrier for everyday citizens to participate in sustainability efforts.

On this platform, anyone can become an Organizer and host activities such as beach cleanups, tree planting, recycling workshops, or community talks. At the same time, Volunteers can explore upcoming events, sign up with one click, and keep track of their personal contribution journey.

What You Can Do Here

  • Discover Green Events: Browse a curated list of eco‑friendly activities happening around you.
  • Create Your Own Campaign: Publish events with clear descriptions, time, location, and categories.
  • Record Your Impact: Volunteers can log participation hours and build a visible record of their efforts.
  • Share Feedback & Stories: Rate events, leave comments, and help organizers improve future activities.
  • Celebrate Contributions: View a leaderboard that highlights active volunteers and inspiring events.

How It Works in Practice

  • Organizers publish events with clear goals (for example, “collect 20 bags of plastic waste from the beach”).
  • Volunteers sign up online and receive event details in advance, which reduces last‑minute confusion.
  • After the event, organizers can update results, such as total hours, amount of waste collected, or trees planted.
  • Participants leave feedback so that each event becomes a learning experience for the next one.

Behind the Platform

  • Technology: Built with Python (Flask), SQLite, HTML/CSS and Jinja2 templates.
  • Design Goals: Simple, friendly interfaces that are easy to understand even for first‑time users.
  • Future Ideas: More detailed statistics, richer event categories, and improved support for long‑term projects.

Thank you for visiting our project. Whether you host an event or join one, every small action brings us one step closer to a greener future.
